GUARANTOR-STATUS OBSERVATION · REVIEWED SUMMARY

Who had a duty to keep the child safe?

A reviewed English summary of the legal question of guarantor status, the public-record boundaries, and the milestones that followed the child’s death.

Translation status. The Traditional and Simplified Chinese editions contain the complete cross-referenced discussion and embedded hearing records. This page does not treat a statement, an inquiry, or a search as a final legal conclusion.

What this legal observation examines

  1. The source and scope of a duty to prevent harm, rather than a person’s job title alone.
  2. The difference between administrative findings, public hearing records, prosecutorial claims, and judicial findings.
  3. Whether the person had information, a protective relationship, a realistic opportunity to act, and a legally relevant omission.
  4. Why institutional workload or role division requires evidence-based analysis and is not automatically a complete defence.
  5. Why final liability remains for the courts to determine under the applicable process.

After the death: 13 public-record milestones

Each entry preserves its legal character. Administrative visits, investigations, searches, indictments and judgments are not interchangeable.

  1. 2023-12-24
    Death and criminal process

    After the child’s death, the case entered criminal investigation and public scrutiny of adoption referral, childcare, and protection systems.

  2. 2023-12-25
    Administrative inquiry begins

    Taipei’s social affairs authority planned an inquiry; the provider was then at a police interview. This is not evidence of a police search.

  3. 2023-12-26
    Childcare-site inquiry

    The authority visited the childcare site and investigated both caregivers. This was an administrative visit, not a warrant search.

  4. 2024-01–02
    Caregivers investigated

    The two caregivers entered criminal investigation and were remanded at different times. Public material does not establish a complete seizure list.

  5. 2024-03-12
    Searches linked to records

    Prosecutors and police searched the Children’s Welfare League and Chen Shang-jie’s residence among other places, preserving relevant devices and work papers; a search is not a finding of guilt.

  6. 2024-03-22
    Institutional inspection

    The Ministry of Health and Welfare and Ministry of Education inspected the organization and identified seven service-process shortcomings.

  7. 2024-04-18
    Main case indicted

    Prosecutors indicted the two caregivers; the direct-abuse case moved into lay-judge proceedings.

  8. 2024-05-01
    Visit rules tightened

    Guidance emphasised unannounced visits, varied times, and seeing the child in person.

  9. 2024-06-03
    Out-of-home care rules revised

    Rules for using home-based childcare for out-of-home placements were clarified, with attention to cross-agency information and escalation.

  10. 2025-05-13
    First-instance main judgment

    The lay-judge court sentenced the two caregivers in the direct-abuse case; this must not be merged with other actors’ liability.

  11. 2025-05-29
    Administrative correction

    The Control Yuan corrected the central ministry and the New Taipei and Taipei city governments; administrative findings have a different legal standard from criminal cases.

  12. 2026-04-16
    First-instance social-worker case

    Chen Shang-jie was sentenced to two years for negligent homicide; both sides appealed, so the judgment was not final.

  13. 2026-07-23
    Main case final

    The Supreme Court rejected the appeals, leaving the direct-abuse sentences final. Other proceedings and reforms still require separate follow-up.
