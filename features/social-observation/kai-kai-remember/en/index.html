Kai-Kai — You came into this world, but could not grow up safely

Kai-Kai was a child who should have been held, fed, soothed, and answered every time he cried.

The facts recognized in the court ruling show a very young child enduring severe harm and prolonged suffering without the ability to seek help or escape. On December 24, 2023, Kai-Kai died.

Remember the child in this photograph. He was not a news item or a case number. May every report, visit, placement, and act of accountability reach the next child in time.
