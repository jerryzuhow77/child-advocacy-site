Fisherman's Wharf outreach for the Kaikai case

For Taiwan and the next generation, please take part if you can.

Time | Thursday, October 22, 2026, 10:30
Venue | Tamsui Fisherman's Wharf

We cannot remain silent about children's safety. The pain of Kaikai's case must not fade with the news cycle. Outreach at Tamsui Fisherman's Wharf will highlight child protection and call for stronger systems and accountable duties so every child can grow up safely and with respect.

We invite everyone who cares about children's rights and reform to take part. Each pause to listen, each shared piece of information, and each voice can help protection move forward.

Please remain peaceful and respectful of on-site rules and other members of the public.
