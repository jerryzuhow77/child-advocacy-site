Public Child-Protection Events in Taipei & New Taipei

This guide lists near-term events with a public attendance or viewing route. Each activity is organized by its named host; check the original notice before attending.

NEW TAIPEI · PUBLIC / ONLINE

29 Oct | 2026 New Taipei International Symposium on Child and Adolescent Mental Health

09:00–16:10 | Hyatt Place New Taipei City Xinzhuang, “Xingchuang 79,” 2F, No. 79, Sec. 3, Xinbei Blvd. | Organizer confirms a simultaneous YouTube livestream

New Taipei City’s Health Department convenes international and cross-disciplinary speakers on digital environments, peer support, school refusal and social withdrawal to strengthen youth mental-health support.

  • Registration & online access: Registration is closed. The organizer’s event page says that the online livestream is open to everyone interested in child and adolescent mental health.
  • Public livestream link: As checked on 28 Oct 2026, no direct, official YouTube watch URL was found on the organizer’s page, the New Taipei Health Department notice, or the official YouTube releases reviewed. The public viewing entry point still awaits confirmation from the organizer; do not infer or guess a URL.
  • Organizer event page: Global Views Monthly
  • Official source: New Taipei Health Department
  • Registrant notice: The event page says registrants who are not approved for in-person attendance will receive an online livestream link three days before the event. That notice is not evidence of a link available to the public.
NEW TAIPEI · FREE PUBLIC EXHIBITION

3–22 Nov | “The Shadow Hidden in Conversations” Online Grooming Prevention Exhibition

Closed Mondays | National Taiwan Library, 1F Lobby, No. 85 Zhong’an St., Zhonghe District, New Taipei City | Free individual admission

Interactive displays help children, young people, caregivers and the public identify online grooming, sexual exploitation and sextortion risks, and learn safety and help-seeking responses.

  • Organizer: ECPAT Taiwan
  • Access: Open to individual visitors; school-group guided visits are available by reservation from 5 Nov on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Fridays.
  • Public notice: Forwarded notice based on ECPAT Taiwan’s letter
  • To confirm: daily opening hours and schedules for parent talks and story sessions.
TAIPEI · PUBLIC PARTICIPATION

7 Nov | World Children’s Day Ecumenical Prayer Gathering: “Safeguarding Children’s Dignity through Faith”

Doors 09:30; 10:00–12:00 | 1F Auditorium, Gengxin Cultural and Educational Institute, No. 22, Sec. 1, Xinhai Rd., Da’an District, Taipei

Music, testimony and shared prayer bring together church, welfare and education communities to affirm children’s dignity and safeguarding.

  • Organizer: Jesuit Safeguarding and Professional Ethics Office
  • Access: Open to people concerned with child safeguarding; the organizer provides registration information and a QR code.
  • Official source: Jesuit Province of China
  • To confirm: registration deadline and capacity.
