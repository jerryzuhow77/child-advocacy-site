Activity Record

On 31 October 2026, we gathered at Tamsui MRT Station and went on to Tamsui Fisherman's Wharf for a peaceful placard action and public advocacy, carrying one message: the Kaikai case must not be forgotten.

This page records only the confirmed date, locations, and advocacy purpose; attendance figures, outcomes, and images that have not been published or verified are not invented or inferred here.

Date | Saturday, 31 October 2026
Gathering time | 12:30 PM
Gathering point | Tamsui MRT Station
Advocacy location | Tamsui Fisherman's Wharf

The action centered on placards and public dialogue, with continued attention to child protection, accountable justice, and institutional reform.

What We Continue to Call For

Children’s safety and dignity must not fade from public view. This action does not reach conclusions about any individual whose responsibility has not been established through due process; it asks every relevant link in the system to face its professional duties, accountability, and necessary improvements.

We seek reforms that can be examined, adequate child-protection resources, and public action that does not leave children behind.
