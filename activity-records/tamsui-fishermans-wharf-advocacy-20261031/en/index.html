Fisherman's Wharf | The Kaikai Case Must Not Be Silenced

One person.s voice may be faint.
But when we stand together, it becomes strength.

We are gathering at Tamsui Fisherman's Wharf so more people can hear Kaikai.s name—and know that people are still willing to speak up for children.

#TheKaikaiCaseMustNotBeSilenced　#WeAreStillHere

Join us. Stand with us and become part of Kaikai.s strength. ❤️🙏

Time | Saturday, 31 October 2026 · meet 12:30
Meeting point | Tamsui MRT Station
Activity area | Tamsui Fisherman's Wharf

People who care about children's rights and institutional reform are invited to join. We will meet at Tamsui MRT Station and proceed to Fisherman's Wharf for peaceful, clear public advocacy.

The activity focuses on placards and gathering. Please take care around traffic and follow on-site conditions.

Please participate peacefully and respect on-site order and other members of the public.

Travel and meeting guidance

Recommended group travel | Bus Red 26: After the 12:30 meeting, the group should take Red 26 from Tamsui MRT Station to “Fisherman’s Wharf.” Official route information lists Red 26 between Fisherman’s Wharf and Tamsui MRT Station, with an off-peak weekend frequency of roughly 10–15 minutes. Check the Greater Taipei Bus live information before travelling.

Missed the meeting? Travel independently: Take Red 26 from Tamsui MRT Station and alight at “Fisherman’s Wharf,” then proceed toward the activity area. Once there, use the LINE community on this page to confirm the group’s current status. The exact on-site rendezvous point will be announced separately by the team; please do not infer it.

Backup | Danhai Light Rail: If Red 26 is disrupted, take the MRT from Tamsui to Hongshulin, then transfer to the Danhai Light Rail for Tamsui Fisherman’s Wharf Station. Because a transfer is required, it is not the preferred group route after meeting at Tamsui.

Check before leaving and stay safe: As of 26 October, no 31 October-specific diversion or traffic-control notice for Red 26 or the Fisherman’s Wharf area has been located. Follow any late operator, police, or traffic-control instructions. Please arrive early, never run for a bus or cross traffic lanes casually, stay with companions, and take care with weather, platforms, and walking around the harbour.
