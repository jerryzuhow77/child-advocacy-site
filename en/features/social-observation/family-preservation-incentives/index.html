Can children safely remain with their families?

24-case policy comparison: Kaikai, Xuān-Xuān, a ten-year-old placement dispute, Nantou Wu child case, Korean placement-child death warnings, and a family-support model. Each examines verifiable system safeguards, not unproven individual liability.

Before separation

Immediate danger requires protection, medical review and safety care. Reducible risk requires a 72-hour safety plan. Missing support requires a 30-day plan that actually starts childcare, respite, housing, health and parenting support.
