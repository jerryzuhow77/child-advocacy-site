Case overview

Liu Zong-xin, chief of Qingshui Police Station in Tucheng Precinct, died after a driver struck and dragged him during a police stop and then hit a guardrail. Prosecutors have brought charges. The defendant’s responsibility and applicable offences must be assessed through court judgments and formal documents.

Case background

Incident and investigationCNA reported on 1 October 2024 that Liu was struck and dragged while checking an illegally parked vehicle and died following a collision with a guardrail. Police later arrested the driver. This is the starting point described in public reporting.
First-instance judgmentCNA reported on 11 February 2026 that the New Taipei District Court’s citizen-judge panel sentenced the defendant to death for murder. The judgment was appealable and was not the final conclusion of the proceedings.
Appeal arguments and evidentiary limitsCNA reported on 15 June 2026 that, during the appeal, the defendant argued that the offence should be classified as injury resulting in death. This is a party’s litigation position, not the court’s final finding. The appellate court will assess the evidence, legal requirements and proceedings.

This page summarizes procedural developments from public reporting. It does not replace judgments, indictments or official court records. The conduct, offences and scope of responsibility remain subject to the court’s final public documents.
