Case background and sources

Tucheng wife-and-sister-in-law killing case

CNA’s 8 October 2026 report says the defendant was accused of striking his wife and sister-in-law with a vehicle and attacking them in Tucheng in July 2025. The first-instance citizen-judge court imposed two life sentences; prosecutors said they would appeal its treatment of the “most serious” crime threshold. The second instance must independently examine the record and arguments.

At the appeal stage, a preparatory hearing is not a final ruling on every factual or legal issue. It can address the shape of the dispute, proposed evidence, written submissions and the sequence of later proceedings. The court’s review of a first-instance ruling is governed by the record and procedural decisions in that particular case. For that reason, this page links back to the court release and the identified news report rather than treating this summary as a substitute for either source. The listed schedule is a site record and must be independently checked against the court’s current notice.

“Two bottles of milk” Jiajia case

The New Taipei District Court release and a CNA report of 25 June 2026 concern a two-year-old child who died after being injured. The first instance imposed life imprisonment on one defendant and 19 years on another. Their sentencing arguments and requests for evidence remain matters for the appeal court.

A short media label cannot carry the elements of criminal responsibility. The appeal court must consider the relevant conduct, causation, mental element, admissibility and any arguments over law or sentence through the formal process. A report that summarizes a first-instance result or a party’s position is useful context, but it does not become the appeal court’s finding. The page therefore limits itself to public sources, avoids details that could further harm a child, and keeps the distinction between a judgment, reporting and litigation argument visible.

Liu Cai-xuan and Liu Ruo-lin: separate case

CNA reported on 27 January 2026 that the two defendants were separately indicted in a case concerning two other young children. This is a distinct proceeding, with different alleged victims and scope, and must not be conflated with the final Kaikai case. The court will organize issues and evidence; responsibility remains for the court to determine.

The public value of a preparatory hearing is that it shows that a case is still being handled through law; it does not authorize the public to assign responsibility in advance. The alleged conduct, responses and evidentiary record in this separate prosecution must be assessed on their own terms. Information that could identify children is not reproduced here. Readers should distinguish an indictment, a defendant’s response, media reporting and an eventual judgment, because each has a different status and none of the first three is a final judicial conclusion.

Station Chief Liu Zong-xin case

PTS reported on 22 October 2026 that station chief Liu died after being dragged by a vehicle and colliding with a barrier while on duty. The first instance imposed the death penalty for murder. On appeal, the parties dispute the legal characterization and evidence, including video review. This page records the public schedule, not a conclusion on criminal liability.

Reporting on a grave public-safety incident naturally focuses on the outcome and its social impact. A criminal appeal, however, must return to the evidence, the accused person’s knowledge and the statutory elements of the offence. How video, witness or expert material should be evaluated is for the court within an adversarial process. Listing the public hearing date does not predict an offence classification, sentence or outcome; it is a prompt to follow the process while withholding conclusions on matters the court has not finally decided.

Chen Shang-jie case

CNA’s 6 November 2026 report and the High Court’s public-attendance notice record that the first instance sentenced Chen to two years for negligent homicide and acquitted her of document forgery. The appeal includes differing views on role, guidance and duty of care. Those litigation positions are not final findings.

Public discussion of child-protection systems can raise important questions about institutional design, but it cannot replace the separate criminal inquiry into the source of a legal duty, available action, foreseeability and causation. Those issues remain for the collegiate court on the record and under applicable law. The page accordingly differentiates official notices, attributed reporting and parties’ positions. It also treats hearing notes as observations rather than an official transcript, and does not turn any summary into a final finding.
