"Review"

Thanks to Mr. Zhang for providing the court record of the first trial of the case of Chen Shangjie, a social worker from the Child Welfare Alliance🙏

This article organizes the key points of the court trial in the form of pictures and texts, and the content is compiled based on the court session to help friends who are concerned about the Kaikai case better understand the details of the case trial.
