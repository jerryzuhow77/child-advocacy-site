ABOUT · HISTORY · ACTION
Introduction to Child Protection Action Alliance｜Historical Events

From the spontaneous actions of caring parents
Walk a path to protect your children

The "Child Protection Action Alliance" is a group of caring parents and volunteer partners in Taiwan who are concerned about the safety of children.

We come from different life backgrounds and met because of our common concern for the protection of children. Everyone uses their time outside work and family to participate in court audits, case support, street publicity, protests and public welfare activities, hoping to record important facts, track judicial procedures, and supervise the implementation of the system, so that the plight of vulnerable families and vulnerable young children can be seen by more people.

OUR HISTORY

Timeline of establishment and development

The name has been adjusted several times, but the original intention of protecting children has never changed.

2025.06

Established the "Love Taiwan Action Network"

Private volunteers began to gather and pay attention to public issues and the safety of children and children.

2025.07

Renamed "Children's Protection Action Group"

Gradually focus on child protection, judicial tracking and street advocacy.

2025.10

Children and Children’s Guardian Facebook fan group officially launched

Establish a community release channel for public information and advocacy content.

2025.12

Instagram and Threads are officially established

Expand your community reach with images, short articles and real-time updates.

2026.08.01

Officially renamed "Child Protection Action Alliance"

Putting children first, system implementation, and continuous action as the common direction.

KEEP GOING

The direction of our continuous efforts

Every date and every action represents that the volunteer partners have been present in person, leaving records for the children and speaking out for justice.

  • Pay attention to major child abuse cases
  • Track judicial proceedings and case progress
  • Collate public trial, verdict and timeline information
  • Examine the child protection system and implementation gaps
  • Promote social attention and reform of children's protection
  • Caring for the Victims and their families
  • Speak up for children who cannot speak for themselves
