How should early warnings trigger protection?

A system should not wait for grave injuries before acknowledging that a child needed help.

Today’s focus
Published: 7 Sep 2026Last checked: 7 Sep 2026, 11:00 (Taiwan)Status: continuously updated

Today’s conclusion: The key question is not only whether a report was made, but whether warnings accumulate, escalate and leave an auditable response record.

Three issues to follow

1
Official judgment

Constitutional Court: juvenile-protection procedure must retain a route to better protection

Where another court can provide more appropriate protection, procedural classification should not override the child’s best interests. The judgment invokes Article 156’s special-protection duty.

2
Audit source · verified

46 of 65: no report or service record in the preceding year

Audit tables 2–3 cover 65 child deaths from intrafamily abuse in 2022–2024. Of these, 46 had no reporting or service record in the preceding year, or 70.77%.

Reading limit: This does not mean there was never a lifetime report, or prove that someone knowingly failed to report every case.
Read audit tables 2–3 ↗
3
Statutory duty · agency guidance

Known protection needs must be reported within 24 hours

Professionals working with children must report immediately, and no later than 24 hours, when they learn that protection may be required. Early warnings should connect welfare, health, education and police before catastrophic injury occurs.

Read MOHW guidance ↗

Four evidence layers

  • Confirmed: the Constitutional Court judgment, audit tables 2–3 and the 24-hour reporting limit.
  • Statistical limit: 70.77% refers only to the preceding year.
  • Media reporting: useful for identifying issues, but figures and responsibility must return to primary sources.
  • Our advocacy: cumulative warnings, inter-agency responses, unannounced visits and supervisory review should form an auditable process.

Advocacy focus

Turn sub-catastrophic warning signs into clear, auditable reporting and protection thresholds that do not depend on one worker’s intuition.
