Protect Childhood, Let Love Take Flight

On October 4, a community child-protection event was held near Henggang Building, Longgang District, Shenzhen. Wish cards, a message wall and hands-on activities invited participants to speak up for children’s safety and rights.

The event also remembered Kaikai and the warning his case carries: children’s distress signals must not be overlooked, and protection work must be visible, handed over responsibly and followed through.

Respect for due process

The case concerning Chen Shang-jie, connected with the Kaikai case, remains in judicial proceedings and is not final. This event record does not prejudge criminal liability. Facts and legal responsibility remain for the court’s final decision.
