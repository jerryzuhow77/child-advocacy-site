VISUAL LANGUAGE

Case Visual Language

The social case area uses dark blue paper sculptures, warm gold titles, white space and restrained images of young children as the common design language. These elements are directly integrated into the layout, cards and paragraphs, rather than relying on a single case poster; without directly showing the injuries, the heaviness of the case and the recognition of the public initiative can be retained.

01Dark blue paper carving layers

Establish a solemn and quiet tone for the case.

02Warm Gold Titles

Make the case title clear but not overly stimulating.

03Symbolic Child Imagery

Replace damage scenes with cribs, toys, lights, etc.

Visual Principles of Cases of Public Concern AreaThe dark blue is not to create oppression, but to bear the weight of the case; the warm gold is only used for the title, thin lines and starlight embellishments; the image of the child is presented with paper silhouettes, guarding hands and soft light sources to avoid curiosity or damaging the picture.
