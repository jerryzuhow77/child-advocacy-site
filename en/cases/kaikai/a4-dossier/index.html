KAIKAI CASE · PEOPLE

The child sent there was just one year old

Case overview and three people involved

Kaikai was a boy a little over one year old. In 2023, while awaiting adoption, he was placed in Liu Cai-xuan’s full-time home-based care through the Child Welfare League Foundation. The court found that from September 1 to December 23, 2023, he endured prolonged, repeated mistreatment and abuse. He died after being taken to hospital on December 24. This page treats the principal abuse case and the social worker’s separate case separately, without conflating different legal responsibilities under one label.
Primary caregiver | Principal abuse case

Liu Cai-xuan

Life imprisonment; lifelong deprivation of civil rights | Final

The court found that she subjected Kaikai to prolonged, repeated and intensive inhumane abuse, treating a defenseless toddler as a means of venting emotion and deriving enjoyment from abuse.

First instance | 2025.05.13Supreme Court | Final on 2026.07.23
Shared care setting | Joint offense

Liu Ruo-lin

18 years’ imprisonment | Final

The court found that she carried out, watched over or supervised the relevant acts within the shared living and care environment. She was not unaware of the treatment shown in photographs and messages, yet participated in the joint offense.

First instance | 2025.05.13Supreme Court | Final on 2026.07.23
CWLF adoption social worker | Defendant in a separate case

Chen Shang-jie

First instance: 2 years for negligent homicide | Not final

At first instance, Taipei District Court found that she held information on Kaikai’s development, changes of care and visits, and could enter the care setting to investigate. Faced with multiple serious anomalies, she failed to investigate further, increase visits or report as required by law. She was acquitted of document forgery at first instance.

THE BROKEN SAFETY NET

Who saw Kaikai?

Three agencies’ responsibilities and cross-system information tree

It is not that “no one was responsible for Kaikai.” Rather, different systems encountered this child at different stages, but each held only its own part of the information.A systemic review must therefore ask not only what one person did, but whether information was actually connected when services changed.
New Taipei Shuying Social Welfare→Child Welfare League Foundation→Taipei Wenshan Home-Based Childcare

All three encountered the same child, but held different perspectives: the family, adoption and childcare oversight.
