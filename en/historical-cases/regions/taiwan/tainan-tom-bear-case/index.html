HISTORICAL CASE · TAINAN · 2012

The Tainan Tom’s World Case

A child’s life must not be reduced to a case label. This page separates publicly verifiable reporting, the final court outcome, and questions for child protection. It does not reproduce graphic details or identifiable images of a child.

Content note
This page concerns violence against and the death of a child. It retains only the facts needed to understand the case and the protection questions it raises.

WHAT IS CONFIRMED

Publicly verifiable milestones

December 2012

A child was killed at a Tom’s World arcade in Tainan, drawing nationwide attention.

26 January 2016

On remand, the Tainan High Court upheld a life sentence; the case remained appealable.

5 May 2016

The Supreme Court dismissed the appeals. The life sentence became final.

READING PRINCIPLE

Keep memory with the child; keep claims with evidence

Public debate may question punishment, psychiatric assessment, and child-protection systems. It should not replace judgments or verifiable sources with emotion. This page distinguishes the court outcome, news reporting, and institutional reflection.

Respect for a child includes refusing to turn their experience into spectacle. The central lesson here is simple: protection must arrive earlier.
