PINNED ACTIVITY RECORD · 2026.08.20

Let Us Write Kaikai’s Story Together

Online story collection · shared remembrance · child-protection advocacy

From the first time we heard Kaikai’s name to following the case, the court proceedings, and the wider conversation about child protection, many of us have carried words left unsaid.

Perhaps there was an image that stayed with you, a question you still cannot resolve, or a new understanding of what it takes to protect a child.

We invite you to leave those thoughts here. Every contribution can preserve love and remembrance for Kaikai—and add one more voice calling for stronger protection for children.

You may wish to share

  • When did you begin following Kaikai’s case?
  • Which development in the case stayed with you most?
  • Which part of the child-protection system most needs to be seen?
  • What new understanding have you gained by following the case?
  • What changes do you hope to see in the future?

Your contribution does not need to be long or complete. A few sentences, a short story, or one concrete proposal are all welcome.

“Can we make sure the next child never has to endure the same tragedy?”
