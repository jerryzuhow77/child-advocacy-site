ACTIVITY RECORD · 2026.08.01

Anti-drug driving and anti-drunk driving on August 1, 2026. Ketagalan Boulevard Parade

Victim’s family support, children’s protection initiative, public participation record

8/1, what we see is not color, nor opposition, nor the grudges between people.

What we see is a group of ordinary Victims' families who, after losing their loved ones, still try to seize every opportunity to seek justice for their families.

The moment they stood on the stage, they shed tears and told what happened to their families. They only hoped that the judiciary would be fair and just and give justice to their families, that's all.

And we, ordinary amateur parents, take to the streets simply to hope that more people will pay attention to child abuse, to pay attention to every child who is a Victim, and to hope that the government can truly see the plight of these children.

However, some people only see positions, only colors, and only their own likes and dislikes, and even only use the number of participants to define the success or failure of an event.

But for this group of family members, being able to accompany each other, understand each other, and stand up bravely is the strength to continue on.

Because only people who have experienced the same pain can truly understand each other, and only they know how difficult it is along the way.

It is everyone's free choice whether to participate in the event; but please respect everyone who is willing to show up to support, and more importantly, respect every family member of the Victim who is still working hard to fight for justice for his family. The value of their coming forward should not be ignored.

What really deserves to be seen is not the number of people or their position, but the fact that this group of family members have still not given up on seeking a fair explanation for their family members to this day.

I hope that every family member of the Victim who is still on the long road to justice can wait for belated justice.

#All the families of the Victims, cheers #HopeTaiwan takes child abuse seriously #Don’t forget your original intention and stay kind
