Overall cause-of-death assessment

03
Explicit wording difference

Malnutrition: aggravating factor or one of the main factors

The record lists malnutrition as an indirect cause/aggravating factor and links it to reduced osmotic pressure and difficulty retaining fluid within vessels.

≠

His team treated malnutrition as a main factor, referring to growth-curve decline, chronic malnutrition, caloric intake and failures of care.

Issue for careful reading

Different ordering does not automatically mean mutual rejection. Its legal significance remains for the court to determine from all evidence.

04
Shared medical foundation

No visible bruise does not mean no deep-tissue injury

Explained that injury may not appear on the skin even when subcutaneous tissue has bled; surface size is only a reference and internal examination matters.

＝

Stated that absence of visible bruising does not mean absence of injury, and that at least 42 injuries were confirmed even though some healed injuries were no longer visible.

Shared point

Both rejected using surface appearance or a single photograph to rule out deeper or earlier injury.

Not only 42 injuries | 42 is the confirmed minimum

The source record states that only some photographs were selected and not every injury photograph was shown. “At least 42 confirmed injuries” is the minimum that could still be identified and confirmed. Some injuries had healed and were no longer visible at death, while injuries including those to the scalp were described as too numerous to list fully. The number 42 must not be read as the complete total of harm the child experienced.

05
Role of CT / PMCT

Imaging supplied evidence, but served different functions

CT could assist but could not replace a forensic doctor’s integrated cause-of-death assessment; some fractures still required dissection and surrounding-tissue examination.

＋

Used PMCT to describe three fractured lower incisors, one missing tooth and root, and a coracoid-process fracture matching the left-shoulder bruising.

How to read it

Imaging is one part of the objective evidence: one expert emphasised its limits, the other used it to test specific injuries.

06
Restraint, standing and oedema

A physiological mechanism could be discussed; exact duration remained fact finding

Explained that prolonged standing could move fluid out of vessels and worsen oedema, while restraint could cause broad blood leakage; the duration of standing could not be determined from the assessment.

＋

Considered the ankle condition more consistent with restraint and said a toddler would not normally remain still for a long period unless extremely frightened.

How to read it

The testimony complements physiology with developmental plausibility; the actual duration and sequence still require other evidence.

07
Testing alternative explanations

Forensic caution and clinical plausibility addressed different questions

On restraint duration, pressure marks, oxygen deprivation and other mechanisms, repeatedly reserved judgment or said the issue required clinical input or factual investigation.

＋

Used pain-protection responses, dental wear patterns and child development to test claims involving self-soothing, head-banging, tooth grinding, stomping or picky eating.

How to read it

The answers were not direct opposites; each discipline addressed a different evidentiary level.

08
Limits of assessment

“Cannot determine” is itself important evidence

Explicitly reserved judgment on medication timing, food quantity, some oxygen-deprivation questions, injury age, pressure marks and duration of standing.

∥

The team excluded disputed items, and he also said short videos could not establish developmental delay and that there was no evidence for a specific tool causing the dental injuries.

Reading reminder

The page preserves these qualifications rather than deleting them because they do not support a simple conclusion.

Reading reminder | comparison is not opposition

The experts had different professional tasks, material and question contexts. Apart from the ranking of malnutrition, most points are shared or complementary; this section does not decide evidentiary weight for the court.

二、基本概念釐清：

屍斑外傷不一定會從皮膚外觀呈現，有時皮下組織有出血，但皮膚卻看不到瘀青皮下組織出血，有可能在組織間擴散瘀傷屬於生前傷，紅斑可能是瘀傷，或是屍斑，或是病變的外觀，或是死後變化（結冰後再解凍）

三、死亡原因：

血液循環（心血管）：心臟—動脈—（組織器官）—微血管—靜脈—心臟

出血→血管破裂使血液流出血管內靜水壓過高：水分易由血管內移至血管外的間質空間血液滲透壓過低：無法將水分留存於血管內循環障礙：血管阻力、姿勢、疾病、受傷、藥物、營養不良、肌肉萎縮等水腫：體內液體過多聚積在細胞間隙組織液定義：存在於間隙組織間的液體，是血液與組織細胞間進行物質交換的媒介，血漿由微血管滲出，成為組織液休克的定義：因身體血管裡的水分或血液不足， 使得身體重要器官沒有足夠的血量，而出現血壓下降的症狀細胞組織灌流不足和細胞缺氧，會導致死亡
