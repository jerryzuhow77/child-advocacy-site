Liu Zongxin Hearing · Attend and Observe

For Taiwan and the next generation, please join us if you can.

Time | Oct 22 (Thu), 15:30
Venue | No. 127, Bo’ai Rd. (Courtroom 1)

Judicial proceedings deserve respect, and children’s rights and public accountability must remain visible. We invite those concerned with child protection, institutional responsibility and justice to observe in person without disrupting court order.

Attendance is not intended to interfere with the trial. It is a calm, rational and resolute way to show that society values children’s safety, institutional accountability and judicial transparency. Please respect courtroom dignity and all security, observation and photography rules.

The actual hearing time, courtroom and entry rules are subject to the court’s announcement on the day. Please reconfirm before leaving.
