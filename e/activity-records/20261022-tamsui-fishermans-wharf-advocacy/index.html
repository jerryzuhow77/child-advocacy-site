Fisherman’s Wharf Outreach · Keep Kai-Kai’s Case in Focus

For Taiwan and the next generation, please join us if you can.

Time | Oct 22 (Thu), 10:30
Venue | Tamsui Fisherman’s Wharf

For children, we cannot remain silent. The pain exposed by Kai-Kai’s case must not be forgotten as news attention fades. We will hold a public outreach event at Tamsui Fisherman’s Wharf so that more people see the importance of child protection and continue to call for stronger systems and meaningful accountability.

We warmly invite people who care about children’s rights and institutional reform to take part. Every person who stops, shares information or speaks up helps move the protection of children forward.

Please remain peaceful and rational, and respect on-site order and other members of the public.
