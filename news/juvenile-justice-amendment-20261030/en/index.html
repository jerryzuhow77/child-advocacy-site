Juvenile-justice amendments move to the legislature

On 29 October, the Executive Yuan approved draft amendments to the Juvenile Delinquency Act for legislative review. They are not law in force.

2026.10.30 · official release 10.29

A confirmed procedural step

On 29 October, the Executive Yuan approved draft amendments to the Juvenile Delinquency Act and Article 18-2 for submission to the Legislative Yuan. This is a bill moving through procedure, not a rule already in force.

What the draft addresses

According to the Ministry of Justice, the package includes victim participation in serious juvenile-crime proceedings, differentiated parole rules and different record-expungement pathways for protection and criminal cases. The Ministry also proposed considering victims’ or bereaved families’ views in parole review.

Executive approval of a draft is not the same as a law in force.

What remains unresolved

Bill text, legislative deliberation, commencement dates and implementation measures can still change. Approval by the Executive Yuan must not be presented as a new rule already applied to existing cases.

Why this matters to child protection

Juvenile justice involves victims’ voices, rehabilitation, record privacy and public safety. The balance needs to be assessed through the published bill, debate and implementation, not a single case or label.

Alliance watch: historical advocacy, not today’s news

No new official alliance statement was found in the past three days. Its 2018, 2025 and 2026 materials remain dated advocacy records with implementation kept separate.
