Why are courtroom statements not a conclusion?

A preparatory hearing helps identify issues and evidence. A claim being made is not the same as a claim being accepted; an allegation is not a conviction. The court still has to decide which rules apply, what facts are proven, and whether legal responsibility is connected to the result.

  • The defendant’s account is a defence position.
  • The prosecution’s account of statutory duties and risk is a prosecutorial position.
  • Evidence and legal evaluation remain for the panel to decide under law.
