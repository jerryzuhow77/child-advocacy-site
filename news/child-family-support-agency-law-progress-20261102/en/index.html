INSTITUTIONAL PROGRESS REVIEW · NOT TODAY’S NEWS

The child-and-family support agency law is promulgated. What must happen next?

The law has passed and been promulgated. The next test is staffing, budgets, cross-system coordination, and whether children can actually reach support.

Read this first: promulgation is not proof of completed operations

Legislative Yuan records show that the Organisation Act for the Ministry of Health and Welfare’s Child and Family Support Agency passed its third reading on 25 August 2026; the Presidential Office promulgated the enactment on 30 September. These are verifiable institutional milestones. This page does not describe promulgation as proof that the new agency is fully operating, nor does it treat an organisational plan as service outcomes.

How this page reads the record

Confirmed: the third reading, promulgation, and the statutory portfolio described by the legislature.
Still to verify: commencement and launch dates; staffing, budgets, internal functions, cross-agency processes and service outcomes.
Not today’s news: every milestone below carries its original date; this review was compiled on 2 November.

Timeline: planning, passage, promulgation

  1. 11 Dec 2025 · planningThe Executive Yuan outlined organisational reform to integrate child protection, care, family support and health policy.
  2. 25 Aug 2026 · third readingThe legislature passed the act. Its public account describes a portfolio spanning child welfare and rights, health promotion and family support, welfare services, and oversight of child welfare institutions.
  3. 30 Sep 2026 · promulgationA presidential order promulgated the law. That confirms the legal step, not that every administrative preparation or service result is complete.

The law offers a coordination framework

The Legislative Yuan described a dedicated body to coordinate child and youth policy and build support from birth through special needs. This matters to protection because children and caregivers often need health, family, welfare and safety support at once. A framework, however, does not automatically remove waiting times, referral gaps or regional inequality.

Legislative resolutions supply a checkable to-do list

The 25 August legislative record called for analysis, resource mapping and policy review on child and family mental-health promotion within six months, followed by a concrete improvement proposal. This is a direction set out in a legislative resolution, not evidence this page can claim has already been completed.

What should be followed next?

  • Commencement: When will operations formally begin, and how will transfers of functions be announced?
  • Capacity: Are staffing, budgets and accountabilities clear and inspectable?
  • Continuity: Is there a usable shared route across protection, care, health, education and family support?
  • Results: Will understandable, de-identified data on access, waiting and outcomes be published?

If help is needed, do not wait for institutional reform

For suspected neglect, physical or psychological abuse, sexual violence or exploitation, use the 113 Protection Hotline, its online consultation route, or Care E; where danger is immediate, contact emergency services first.
