CHILD PROTECTION POLICY WEEKLY · 2026-11-22

Weekly policy report | No major new rule in force

This report checks official notices issued from 15 to 22 November on child protection, adoption services and the justice system. It does not treat a web-page refresh or a policy discussion as a new law. It separates rules in force, work still under development, and information not yet supported by enough primary evidence for publication.

What can be confirmed this week

No material new child-protection rule already in force was confirmed this week that requires an immediate rewrite of the site’s legal explanations, social-work responsibility analysis or case features. That does not mean the system needs no change; it means the currently public primary sources do not support presenting an intended direction as an operative duty.

How the sources were read

The MOHW Protection Services Department regulations page showed an update on 20 November, but the listed child-protection rules and guidance remained existing items. A page refresh is a lead for follow-up, not proof by itself that a new rule has taken effect.

The Judicial Yuan’s 5 November committee update addresses trauma-informed justice, limitation periods for child victims of sexual violence, and privacy in judgments. Those are important follow-up issues, but are not newly effective rules in this reporting week.

Effect on site content

No mandatory legal-field correction is identified this week for the Taiwan or Hong Kong sites in any language. Articles discussing legislative amendment, guidance review or cross-network cooperation should continue to distinguish proposals, policy direction and rules already in force.

Next checks

The next review will watch formal notices from MOHW, the Social and Family Affairs Administration, local authorities and the Judicial Yuan. If a text is promulgated, a commencement date announced, or a concrete operational guideline issued, the next report will list old-versus-new differences and the fields awaiting four-language updates.
