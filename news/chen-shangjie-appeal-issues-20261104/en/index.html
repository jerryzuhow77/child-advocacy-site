HOW TO READ

Start with the confirmed case numbers and hearing time, then read the three questions. This page does not infer undisclosed appeal grounds, evidence or courtroom submissions.

01 · DUTY

Duty of care: what did the information then available require?

The question is how records of contact, visits and medical follow-up are assessed within the professional role, authority and resources available at the time—not only through hindsight.

Outcome cannot replace an item-by-item assessment of contemporaneous risk information and options.

02 · CAUSATION

Causation: would earlier action have changed the result?

Even if a lapse is identified, the court must separately examine whether a specific earlier act could have timely activated reporting, medical or protective processes, and whether evidence supports that link.

What should have been done and whether doing it would have avoided the result are distinct questions.

03 · EVIDENCE

Appeal scope: which findings will be tested?

Watch the actual grounds of appeal, contested records and any new evidence. A preparation hearing organizes issues and evidence; it is not a judgment.

Unpublished submissions should not be reported as settled facts.
