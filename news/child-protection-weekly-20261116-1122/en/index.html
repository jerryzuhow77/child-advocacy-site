Taipei | Tuition refund mediation to proceed on 27 November

On 19 November, Taipei’s Department of Legal Affairs said the provider in the Charlise tuition refund dispute had indicated it would decline to attend mediation scheduled for 27 November. After parents asked the city to keep the original date, the department confirmed on 21 November that the meeting would go ahead. It scheduled three days of briefings from 23 November on procedures, documents and further avenues for seeking a remedy. The provider’s attendance and any refund agreement remain open. The department will continue assessing whether it can help parents pursue a group lawsuit.

When a child moves from school to after-school care or community services, is there a clear point of contact? After a referral, who checks that the family actually receives the service? Tainan | From helping plan an event to receiving a written response

On 20 November, Tainan City reported that its eighth cohort of child and youth representatives helped plan a World Children’s Day event and led activity stations. The next thing to watch is whether the city publishes their proposals, names the responsible offices and explains which proposals it adopts and why. That record would let participants see what happened to their input.
