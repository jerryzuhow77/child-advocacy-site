System Watch | When a child enters family court, who protects a place for their voice?

Training is not proof of impact. The real question is whether a child can be heard safely, clearly and with a meaningful response.

Published 29 Oct 2026 · notice 27 Oct · training 12–13 Nov

What is confirmed

A 27 October notice announced a Judicial Yuan-commissioned, 13-hour specialist programme on children and procedural guardians. Its two days of sessions cover court practice, child interviews and views exploration, case analysis, and report writing.

The legal role: not a voice for either parent

Under Taiwan’s Family Act, a procedural guardian may be appointed where a child’s interests may conflict with those of a legal representative, representation is difficult, or the court finds it necessary to protect the child’s interests. In matters concerning a minor child’s rights and duties, the court may appoint one on application or on its own motion.

A procedural guardian is not an advocate for either parent, nor does every sentence a child says become the outcome of a case. The role is to support expression in conditions that are suitable, child-friendly and free from pressure, while analysing a child’s views separately from the child’s best interests.

A child is not an accessory to an adult dispute. Asking for their view does not hand them the burden of deciding.

What to follow beyond the training hours

  1. Whether courts across regions have enough specialists with child-focused expertise.
  2. Whether interviews avoid leading questions, loyalty conflicts and repeated retelling.
  3. Whether reports distinguish a child’s words, professional observations and evaluative analysis.
  4. Whether the child’s views receive a reasoned response rather than simple recording.
  5. Whether rural, disabled and linguistically or culturally diverse children receive equal procedural support.

Verification boundary

The notice, dates, venue, course direction and statutory framework are verified. They do not establish that every child-related family case will have a procedural guardian, that training has improved case quality, or that courts must decide exactly as a child expresses a preference.
