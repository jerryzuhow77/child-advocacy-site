When a clinic visit becomes a protection network

The 27 October Jingwang Awards spotlighted reporting, accompaniment, referral and justice-sector coordination. Recognition is not proof that every protection gap is closed.

2026.10.29 · ceremony 10.27 · report 10.28

What is newly confirmed

Taiwan Against Violence Alliance held the 14th Jingwang Awards ceremony on 27 October. A 28 October report says the Qiaotou District Prosecutors Office and several Kaohsiung network partners received an excellence award for a child-protection collaboration model. This page focuses on recognition and workflow, not identifiable information or injury details.

From one warning sign to several professions: the reported hand-off

The report describes a relative seeking help after noticing unusual weight loss in an infant; a social worker visited and accompanied medical care; clinicians identified risk and referred onward; investigators and a child-protection medical team then assessed the evidence. Each role is distinct, and a delay at any point can leave risk in place.

A protection network is a hand-off, not a single institution.

What the award recognises — and what it cannot establish

The award shows that this collaboration was recognised. It does not establish that systems are complete everywhere, and it cannot substitute for a full case record or a court judgment.

If you see a possible risk

Call the police if there is immediate danger. For suspected child abuse, neglect or a child who needs protection, Taiwan’s 113 protection hotline is available. Do not circulate images, identify people online or treat reposting as reporting.

Alliance watch: historical advocacy, not relabelled as today’s news

No new official statement from the alliance was found in the past three days. Its 2018, 2025 and 2026 materials remain historical advocacy records, not proof of implementation.

Sources and verification limits
