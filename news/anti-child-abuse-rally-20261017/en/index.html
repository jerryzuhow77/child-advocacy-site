Stop abuse! Protect children!
Join us on Ketagalan Boulevard on October 17!

To everyone who cares about children: I am Hsu Li-hsin, a Taipei City councillor.

A recent kindergarten abuse case in my constituency involved corporal punishment, intimidation, and mistreatment. Our appeal identifies a gap: childcare centres for ages 0–2 have mandatory camera rules, while kindergartens for children over two lack a clear legal basis for such requirements.

Child protection must have no gaps. Hsu Li-hsin invites you to speak up for children and frontline early childhood educators.

Time | Saturday, October 17, 2026, 15:30Venue | Ketagalan Boulevard, Taipei

Our demands

  1. Establish a legal basis for camera equipment in kindergartens serving children over two.
  2. Set clear installation, retention, and access rules.
  3. Establish local-government inspection and oversight.
  4. Preserve footage lawfully and establish facts when concerns arise.

Stand together for children's safe growth!
