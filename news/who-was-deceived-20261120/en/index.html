PINNED ANALYSIS · CASE TIMELINE

Who was misled? Who withheld information?

A public-interest timeline examining when injuries, lost teeth, and information to the grandmother were known and communicated.

Procedural note: This page organizes materials supplied by advocates and public defence arguments. Disputed facts, evidence, and individual legal responsibility remain for the court to determine.

Injuries, successive tooth loss, and changes in a child’s condition are not abstract issues. The public-interest question is when warning signs were seen, who held the information, and when the family was informed.

Timeline of information

    According to the materials used on this page, a large forehead bruise was seen during a visit.

  2. 24 Oct 2023

    A photograph was sent to the grandmother about a month later

    The injury was described as an accidental impact while playing in a park.

  3. 19 Nov 2023

    Three teeth reportedly fell out in one night

    The page’s materials say this information was conveyed to the social worker and was not immediately relayed to the grandmother.

  4. 7 Dec 2023

    A fourth tooth reportedly fell out

    The page’s materials say a further report was made to the social worker.

  5. 9 Dec 2023

    The grandmother was told two days later

    The materials describe the tooth loss being relayed together with trust in the caregiver.

“The caregiver really loved and cared for the child, so I believe there was no problem with their care.”

When a defence argument says someone was misled or information was withheld, the timeline still raises questions: Can an injury personally observed be attributed only to another person’s withholding? Why were relatives not immediately informed after reports of tooth loss? What risk assessment supported continued trust?

These are questions for public scrutiny; final evidence assessment and legal responsibility remain with the court.
