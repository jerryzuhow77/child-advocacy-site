A draft moves forward, but is not law in force

On 29 October, the Executive Yuan approved draft amendments to the Juvenile Delinquency Act submitted jointly with the Judicial Yuan. Official material covers victim participation, parole thresholds, record expungement and procedures for juvenile–adult co-offence cases; the Judicial Yuan says the drafts will go to the legislature.

Cabinet approval and the move to legislative review are confirmed; the draft is not in force. Implementation, safeguards and any textual changes remain for legislative review and later public records.

Mental-health support: a forum concluded; outcomes still need evidence

New Taipei’s Department of Health reported on 29 October that its youth mental-health forum had concluded, covering resilience, peer support, digital communities and cross-sector coordination. The event, topics and stated measures are verifiable; reach, referral continuity and outcomes need later data and independent evaluation.

This site’s weekly record and action information

This site separately tracked the juvenile-justice drafts and New Taipei’s support network. This roundup adds no unsupported facts; it connects procedural rights and everyday support. For suspected neglect, abuse, sexual violence or exploitation, use the 113 Protection Hotline, online consultation or Care E reporting; use emergency services first where danger is immediate.
