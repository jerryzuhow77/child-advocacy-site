Beyond refunds:
remedy must remain connected

A refund may form one part of a civil remedy. It cannot replace investigation, accountability, or a child’s recovery support.

Three things to separate

What does “mediation” address?

Taipei’s Legal Affairs Department says the requested mediation concerns contracts, performance, fees and civil claims. It is distinct from criminal investigation and administrative action.

Who determines responsibility?

Administrative and criminal processes have different evidence and timetables. A party’s account or one procedural update is not a final determination.

Support should not wait.

Clear information, records and evidence preservation, recovery support and accessible routes to compensation should move alongside the process.

The question is not only whether a meeting happens

Public reporting indicates that the 27 November mediation arrangement and support measures remain under attention. Can families obtain understandable procedural information? Are children receiving sustained, appropriate recovery support? Can this experience lead to earlier recognition and faster intervention?
