校園性別事件：程序如何守住安全？

教育部北區校園性別事件行政訴訟案例研討會於 11 月 12 至 13 日舉行。研討會已核實；個案處理品質、撤銷率或學生安全是否改善，不能僅由培訓推論。

已核實：這兩天在做什麼？

教育部公告，115 年度校園性別事件行政訴訟案例研討會分區辦理三場；北區場於 11 月 12 至 13 日在世新大學進行。對象包括大專校院性別平等教育委員會委員、執行秘書、調查專業人才庫人員及相關諮詢委員。

今年每場由原本一日擴充為兩日。公告列出的內容包括行政訴訟案件的程序與實體爭點、撤銷案例的分組研討，以及從權力關係、權勢控制與專業倫理出發的議題討論。

程序不是附帶工作

校園性別事件的處理，關係到當事學生能否在安全、尊重且不受歧視的環境繼續受教。「依法處理」不只是做完表格或開完會；調查、通知、陳述與救濟的每一步，都會影響權益是否被理解與保護。

教育部說明，案例研討特別聚焦行政程序法與行政訴訟判例涉及的適法性及論理脈絡，目的在降低調查結果遭行政訴訟撤銷的風險。這項方向可核實；但不應把「降低撤銷」誤讀為只求程序形式正確。程序的目的仍是讓事實、權力差異與當事人的受教權能被公平看見。

培訓之後，該追問什麼？

一場研討會不等於所有校園都已具備相同處理能力。後續值得監督的包括：調查人員是否充足且獨立、學生是否能取得清楚且年齡合宜的資訊、保護措施是否及時、申訴與救濟是否可近，以及學校是否以去識別化方式公開可檢驗的制度改善資料。

專業培力是必要起點，不是成效證明。 對兒少而言，真正重要的是在需要求助時，能否得到不被責難、可理解且持續的支持。

程序不是附带工作

校园性别事件处理关系到学生能否在安全、尊重且不受歧视的环境继续受教。依法处理不只是完成表格或开会；调查、通知、陈述与救济都会影响权益是否被看见与保护。

School gender-based incidents: how can procedure safeguard safety?

The Ministry of Education’s northern regional workshop on administrative-litigation cases ran on 12–13 November. The training is verified; it alone does not show that case quality, reversal rates, or student safety have improved.

School safetyDue processPolicy watch

What is verified?

The Ministry states that its 2026 workshop series on administrative-litigation cases involving school gender-based incidents has three regional sessions. The northern session was held at Shih Hsin University on 12–13 November for equality-committee members, executive secretaries, investigation specialists and advisers.

Each session was expanded from one to two days. The programme includes procedural and substantive issues in administrative litigation, discussion of decisions setting aside investigations, and reflection on power relations, coercive control and professional ethics.

Procedure is not a side task

How a school responds can affect whether a student can continue learning in safety, dignity and without discrimination. A lawful process is more than forms or meetings: investigation, notice, participation and remedies all shape whether rights are understood and protected.

The Ministry says the workshop examines legality and reasoning in administrative-procedure and litigation cases, including ways to reduce the risk of an investigation being set aside. That is a verified aim, not proof of outcomes. Reducing reversals must never mean treating procedure as a mere formality.

What should follow?

Training does not guarantee equal capacity across schools. Follow-up should ask whether investigators are sufficient and independent, whether students receive clear age-appropriate information and timely protection, whether remedies are accessible, and whether institutions publish de-identified evidence that reforms can be assessed.

Professional development is a necessary starting point, not evidence of success.

Verification: the regional workshop, dates, venue, intended participants and announced themes are verified.
Not established: details of any individual case, lower reversal rates, or improved school safety.
